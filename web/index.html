    Master Splinter's Wisdom

    Attend, my students.

    This game, Othello, is a contest of balance and foresight. Two players place discs upon the board, black and white. When you place a disc so that it traps your opponent's pieces between yours, those pieces become yours as well.

    You must always make such a move if one is available. When neither player can move, the contest ends. The one who controls the greater number of discs is victorious.

    Remember—victory does not come from haste, but from patience and wisdom.
